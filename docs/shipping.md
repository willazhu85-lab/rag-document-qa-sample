# Shipping and Delivery

## Rates and timing
Standard shipping costs $8 for orders under $75 and is free for orders of $75 or more before tax. Standard orders ship within 2 business days and usually arrive 4–7 business days after dispatch in the contiguous United States.

## Expedited and remote destinations
Two-day shipping costs a flat $22 and must be selected at checkout. Alaska, Hawaii, and PO boxes are excluded from the 4–7 business day estimate; those destinations usually take 7–12 business days. Delivery dates are estimates and severe weather may cause delays.
