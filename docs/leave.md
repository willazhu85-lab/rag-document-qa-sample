# Time Off

## Vacation allowance
Full-time employees receive 15 paid vacation days per calendar year, prorated for their start date. Vacation requests should be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance and require manager approval.

## Carryover and sick leave
Up to 5 unused vacation days may carry into the next year and must be used by March 31. Employees receive 6 paid sick days per year. Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive workdays requires a healthcare provider note.
