# Product Safety and Recalls

## Recall notices
Customers should stop using any product identified in an Acme recall notice and follow the notice instructions for return or disposal. Acme posts active recalls on its website and contacts registered purchasers by email when contact details are available.

## Incident reporting
Report a product safety incident to safety@acme.example with the product name, purchase date, and a description of the incident. For an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services first. Do not continue using a product that appears structurally damaged.
