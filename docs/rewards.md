# Trail Rewards

## Earning and redeeming points
Members earn 1 point per whole dollar spent on eligible merchandise, excluding tax, shipping, and gift cards. Every 100 points may be redeemed for a $5 discount. Points post 14 days after an order ships.

## Expiration and limits
Points expire after 12 months without qualifying purchase activity. Rewards cannot be exchanged for cash, and no more than $25 in rewards may be applied to one order. Returned merchandise reverses the points earned on that merchandise.
