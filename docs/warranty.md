# Product Warranty

## Coverage periods
Tents and backpacks have a 2-year limited warranty from the purchase date. Sleeping bags have a 1-year limited warranty. Warranty coverage applies to defects in materials or workmanship and requires proof of purchase.

## Exclusions and claims
Normal wear, accidental damage, improper care, and products purchased from unauthorized sellers are excluded. Customers submit a claim with the order number and photos; approved claims are repaired or replaced at Acme's discretion. Warranty coverage does not extend the original period after a replacement.
