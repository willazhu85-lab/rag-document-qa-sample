# Wholesale Orders

## Eligibility and pricing
Wholesale accounts require business registration and a minimum opening order of $1,000. Approved accounts receive a 20% discount from current list price. Reorders have a $500 minimum.

## Payment and lead time
Wholesale invoices are due within 30 days of shipment. Wholesale orders are final sale and typically require 10 business days to prepare before dispatch. Discounts cannot be combined with promotional codes.
