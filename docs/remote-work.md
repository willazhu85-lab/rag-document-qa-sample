# Remote Work

## Eligibility and schedule
After 90 days of employment, eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Customer-support staff must maintain their published shift coverage regardless of work location.

## Equipment and security
Acme provides one laptop and a $300 one-time home-office allowance after remote-work approval. Employees must use the company VPN for internal systems and may not store customer data on personal devices.
