# Customer Privacy

## Data retention and access
Order records are retained for 7 years for accounting and customer-service needs. Customers may request a copy or correction of account information by contacting the Acme privacy team; Acme responds within 30 days after verifying the requester's identity.

## Marketing preferences
Customers may unsubscribe from promotional email using the link in any marketing message. Transactional order and safety notices are still sent when necessary to provide service or protect customers.
