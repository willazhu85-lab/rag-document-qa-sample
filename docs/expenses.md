# Employee Expense Reimbursement

## Submission deadline and limit
Employees must submit business expenses within 30 days of purchase using the expense form and an itemized receipt. Manager approval is required for every reimbursement. Meals are reimbursed up to $45 per person per day.

## Travel and excluded expenses
Mileage for approved business travel is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile; commuting is not reimbursable. Alcohol, traffic fines, and expenses without a receipt are not reimbursed. Finance pays approved claims in the next monthly payroll cycle.
