# Promotions and Discounts

## Coupon rules
Only one promotional code may be used per order. Coupons do not apply to gift cards, taxes, or shipping charges unless the offer explicitly says otherwise. Expired codes cannot be reinstated.

## Price adjustments
Acme may grant a one-time price adjustment if an item goes on sale within 7 days after purchase. The request must be made within that 7-day period, and the item must still be available for sale.
